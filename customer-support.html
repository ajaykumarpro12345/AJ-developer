Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a WebView APK and a Full Native APK?

A WebView APK packages your responsive website into an installable Android app so any content updates on your website appear immediately inside the app. A Full APK provides a complete native Android experience with custom navigation, offline support, and deeper device integrations.

What types of websites does AJ Developers build?
